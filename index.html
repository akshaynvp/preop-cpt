Author

Akshay Pulavarty, MD, MPH
Dermatology Resident, NYU Langone Health

This tool was built to assist dermatology residents and surgical team members with pre-operative CPT code estimation for common dermatologic surgical procedures.

Code outputs are estimates only and should be verified against current CPT guidelines and institutional billing compliance policies before submission. The tool does not constitute legal, financial, or medical advice.

CPT codes were cross-referenced against, and verified with, the CPT 2027 Professional Edition (American Medical Association). Codes follow CPT and Medicare NCCI conventions (2026) and are reviewed each year when CPT is updated. Outputs are preoperative estimates: confirm each code against the procedure performed and documented. Payer and MAC policies vary and are not modeled.

For questions or feedback, contact: akshaynvp@gmail.com

Change Log

v1.25November 16, 2026
  • New Biopsy mode (third toggle). ICD-10 D48.5 (neoplasm of uncertain behavior of skin) is the default; no site or size is needed for skin biopsies.
  • Skin biopsy: enter the number of shave, punch and incisional biopsies. Codes follow the CPT 2027 multiple-biopsy rules (11102/11103, 11104/11105, 11106/11107); 88305 is shown per specimen.
  • Excisional biopsy: choose the anatomic group and excised diameter to get the excision code (benign series, with the malignant series if final pathology is malignant). Repair is reported separately.
  • Special-site biopsy: ear 69100, lip 40490, eyelid skin including lid margin 67810, nail unit 11755, penis 54100, vulva/perineum 56605 + 56606.
v1.24November 16, 2026
  • Shortened the note under clinical lesion diameter.
v1.23November 15, 2026
  • New count-based Destruction mode (toggle at the top of the form). Actinic keratosis: 17000 for the first lesion, 17003 for each of the second through 14th, 17004 for 15 or more. Benign lesions other than skin tags and vascular lesions: 17110 (up to 14) or 17111 (15 or more). No size or site entry.
  • Special-site destruction, simple and extensive codes shown together: anus (46900, 46910, 46916, 46917; 46924), penis (54050, 54055, 54056, 54057; 54065), vulva (56501; 56515), eyelid lid margin (67850). Codes follow the CPT 2027 cross-reference for destruction in specific anatomic sites.
  • Descriptors now match the CPT 2027 wording: mucous membrane added to the face series for benign excision (11440–11446, “other benign lesion”), shave removal (11310–11313) and malignant destruction (17280–17286); all 88305 pathology descriptors use the level IV skin wording.
  • A copy of the current version is saved as index.html with every release.
v1.22November 15, 2026
  • Lesion diameter is now optional. Leave it blank for skin tags and other count-based procedures: the tool returns the ICD-10 code, the size-independent CPT codes (skin tag removal, benign destruction) and pathology. Size-banded codes (excision, shave, malignant destruction, repair) are listed as code ranges marked "needs size" instead of a single code. Mohs AUC, margin and SLNB guidance need a size and are not shown when it is blank.
v1.21October 4, 2026
  • CPT tables rewritten as explicit code lists. Removed nonexistent codes 11405/11425/11445, 11605/11625/11645 and 17265/17275/17285; lesions over 4.0 cm now map to the x6 codes.
  • Anatomic groups now follow CPT descriptors rather than the AUC area. Forehead, cheek and chin use the face series; ankle uses 12031; complex repair uses the 13100, 13120, 13131 or 13151 family by site.
  • Complex repair over 7.5 cm now lists the primary code plus add-on units; no complex code is offered below 1.1 cm (13150 is not in CPT 2027 and was removed). Same add-on logic for grafts and flaps; 15220, 14020/14021 and 14301/14302 added.
  • Benign excisions of 0.5 cm or less no longer list repair codes (NCCI).
  • Copy Codes now copies one billing alternative at a time; excision, repair and destruction are no longer combined.
  • Skin tag removal now uses 11200/11201. ICD-10: corrected eyelid codes; added lip codes for melanoma and MIS; SK and skin tag codes.
  • Pathology levels now follow the CPT specimen list: cyst, tag and lipoma 88304; nevus, SK, DF and skin cancers 88305; DFSP and leiomyosarcoma 88307.
  • All CPT codes cross-referenced against the CPT 2027 Professional Edition (AMA).
